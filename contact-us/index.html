Contact Us

We're always looking for active and enthusiastic new members to join our friendly, volunteer-run club.

If you're interested in becoming a member, contact us by emailing: argonautsmembership@gmail.com or pop along to one of our our weekly meetings. We meet every other Tuesday evening:

  • Between 6.15-7.00pm you can find us in the diving pool at the Queen Mother Sports Centreon Vauxhall Bridge Road.
  • From 7.00pm onwards on the pool session evening, we can be found either at the back or upstairs at the Constitution pub, 42 Churton Street, Pimlico, London, SW1V 2LP. It's a few minutes walk from the sports centre, and if you can't find us, please ask at the bar.
  • On non-pool session evening, we can be found either at the back or upstairs at the Constitution pub from around 6.15pm onwards.

Please contact us by emailing: argonautsmembership@gmail.com to find out on which Tuesday we are having a pool session.

You can also get in touch via our Facebook page!
